About Me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in the Department of Accounting at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, and I am on the 2026–2027 academic job market.

My research studies how firms and professionals interact with the public sector, and how making those interactions visible changes behavior on both sides.

My job market paper shows that firms react to the public exposure of an individual-firm connection by reducing voluntary financial disclosure, driven by the connected individual's privacy cost.

Before joining the HKUST Ph.D. program, I earned an M.Sc. in Accounting from HKUST and a B.Sc. in Business Administration and Management from Bocconi University.

Research Areas: Disclosure and Transparency · Corporate Governance · Political Economy
